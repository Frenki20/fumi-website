FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Send us a message and we’ll reply within 24 hours.

Can I choose just one service instead of a package?

Yes. Every service is also available on its own — see Individual Services above — or ask us for a custom quote that combines exactly what you need.

Can I switch to a different plan later?

Yes. Start with the plan that fits your business today and move up as you grow — just let us know.

Do you use templates?

No. Every website, identity and campaign is designed from scratch for your brand, your audience and your goals.

Do you work with businesses outside Albania?

Yes. We work with local businesses and international brands alike, in English or Albanian.

How quickly will you reply?

Within 24 hours. Message us through the form, by email or on WhatsApp and we’ll come back with ideas and clear next steps.

Why are prices shown in euros and lekë?

Our prices are set in euros. The amounts in lekë are approximate, based on a recent exchange rate, to give you a quick idea of the cost.

04About Us

A creative partner for brands with big ambitions.

FUMI is a digital marketing and web development agency built on a simple belief: every business deserves a digital presence as strong as its vision.

We’re a team of strategists, designers and developers who obsess over the details — because the details are what make people trust, click and come back. We combine the creativity of a design studio with the focus of a performance agency.

We work as an extension of your team: transparent, hands-on and invested in your results. Whether you’re a local business taking its first big step online or an international brand ready to level up, we bring the same energy, care and ambition to every project.

Our Vision

To be the creative partner ambitious brands trust to shape their digital future — wherever in the world they are.

Our Mission

To help businesses grow by turning their ideas into clear strategies, striking design and digital experiences that deliver measurable results.
