Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This page explains what personal information FUMI collects through this website, why we collect it and what rights you have.

Who we are

FUMI is a digital marketing and web development agency based in Albania. You can reach us at FuMi.Agency17@gmail.com or on +355 67 573 1321.

What we collect

  • When you send the contact form: your name and email address, plus anything else you choose to share, such as your company, budget, the services you’re interested in and your message.
  • When you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp or Instagram: the details you send us there.

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. It stores one small setting in your browser for the length of your visit, so the intro animation only plays once.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to work with you. We never sell it, share it for marketing or add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

  • The contact form is delivered by FormSubmit, which forwards your message to our email inbox.
  • Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).
  • The WhatsApp and Instagram links take you to services run by Meta, which have their own privacy policies.
  • Our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to a project are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as we need them for the work and for accounting and legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

You can also contact the Information and Data Protection Commissioner of Albania (IDP) if you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
